Yellow-naped Amazon Parrot
Kingdom: Animalia
Phylum: Chordata
Class: Aves
Order: Psittaciformes

The Yellow-naped Amazon Parrot is a critically endangered bird species found only in the tropical rainforests of South America.

It is a small, colorful bird with distinctive plumage and is known for its ability to mimic human speech.

Due to habitat loss and illegal trafficking, the Yellow-naped Amazon Parrot is one of the rarest birds in the world.
